# Methodology (placeholder; Sim to complete)
- Scores combine soil protection, water suitability and climate resilience using farmer-set weights.
- Scenario water scores respond to growing-season P − reference ET relative to the typical scenario.
- P − reference ET is a simplified indicator, not soil moisture or irrigation need.
- Replay is a historical stress test, not a forecast. Outputs are modeled suitability, not yield or profit.
